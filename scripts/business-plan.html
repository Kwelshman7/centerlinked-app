What did not change

  • Work-email gate. Invite-oriented growth.

Honest status. Product-complete, commercially early. Listing and Search do not require a paid plan. Membership and Done For You are live in Stripe. The app does not yet block adding facilities above a subscribed count.

The job

When a professional is ready to place a client, they need four facts fast: level of care, insurance fit, location, and a real person to call — and those facts still have to be true next month. Today those facts live in PDFs, brochures, and consumer directories. CenterLinked is one live organization URL, plus Search, so the handoff is trustworthy to reopen.

Who this is for

Pays

The treatment organization, when it chooses membership or Done For You. Checkout is org admin only.

Operates

BD, admissions, marketing. Work email. Free accounts can search without claiming an org.

Hospitals, case managers, therapists, other BD reps, probation officers. They reopen the link — or Search.

The leave-behind

Public URL

Organization sheet at /o/:slug. Program sheet per location. One-facility orgs skip the overview and open the program page. Make a Referral calls, texts, or emails the named BD contact.

Trust cadence

Fresh ≤ 30 days · Recent 31–60 · Stale 61–90 · Frozen drops out of Search. A facility stamp is not payer-contract proof. Weekly verification reminders already run.

  • Work-email join at /join; skip-to-Search

  • Org results with facility cards
  • Structured insurance contracts, reusable BD owners
  • PDF import with review-then-save
  • Launch-import for assisted setup
  • Org branding, one-pagers, OG social previews

Community Feed and Messenger exist in the codebase. FEATURES.community is false. Do not turn them on until sheets and Search are clearly the habit.

Positioning

Not a directory for families. A live referral profile for professionals — findable in Search, shareable as one URL. There is no serious incumbent whose only job is that. The category has to be explained, not captured.

CenterLinked Inc.Market and go-to-market

Market we are actually in

Industry backdrop, not CenterLinked data: IBISWorld counts on the order of 5,128 residential mental-health / substance-abuse businesses (2026). Licensed SUD facilities have been estimated around 17,000. Those mix facilities with organizations.

Software TAM is U.S. behavioral-health orgs that take professional referrals and would pay to keep a live profile current — or at least stay listed. A defensible org-count is 5,000–15,000. This is a focused vertical, not “all healthcare.” We are not sizing consumer “rehab near me.”

If 400 orgs pay

All 1-facility at $99/mo ≈ $475k ARR. Mix of 50% at 1 / 35% at 5 / 15% at 10 ≈ $943k ARR.

Scenarios, not forecasts. Listed orgs are not paying orgs.

ICP vs anti-ICP

ICP: multi-location groups that already print collateral and lose deals to stale insurance lists. Anti-ICP: anyone who wants CenterLinked to “get us patients from Google.”

How we go to market

  1. 1Free Search as the habit. Join at /join, skip org claim, run an insurance + geography query the same day.
  2. 2Free listing as catalog. Density in 1–2 metros still beats a national slogan.
  3. 3Founder-led BD. The URL, the one-pager, and Make a Referral are the conference leave-behind.
  4. 4Done For You / launch-import for busy CEOs, priced so a 10-location build is not $499.
  5. 5Convert listed → paid when the live link is clearly their professional infrastructure. Do not pretend Search is paid-only while it is free.

Do not buy “rehab near me.” Do not promise to fill beds.

CenterLinked Inc.18 months · risk · line

Recommended operating plan

Months 0–6

Density and Search habit. Professionals into Search the same day. Orgs listed via self-serve, PDF, or DFY. Community stays off.

Success: a real insurance + geography answer in the first metro.

Months 6–12

One or two geographies. Hold verify-on-time as listing grows. Sell annual. Land multi-site groups above $99.

Success: ZIP/city queries useful; completeness up, not just org count.

Months 12–18

Named date for the free/paid boundary. Enforce facility-count caps if mix shows gaming. Revisit community only after the habit is real.

Success: listed→paid conversion and 5+ facility revenue mix, from real baselines.

The line we will not cross

Consumer treatment directory. Patient intake, scheduling, medical advice, or clinical decision support. PHI. Open consumer lead-gen. Social network as the Phase-1 core. Paid Search placement. Inventing facility addresses or BD contacts to make an import look complete.

Team, financials, funding. Not in the product repo. Do not send this plan to a third party with invented headcount, burn, runway, or a raise amount. Fill Stripe MRR, listed vs paying mix, and the free/paid date before investor or lender use.
